Social function: to persuade readers that something is important to consider, using logical arguments.
PartContent
ThesisIntroduction of the topic and the writer's position/opinion
ArgumentsReasons: point + elaboration
ReiterationRestatement of the opinion (and a recommendation)

Model text

Should Students Be Allowed to Bring Smartphones to School?

Thesis Smartphones have become part of teenagers' daily life. In my opinion, students should be allowed to bring smartphones to school as long as clear rules are applied.

Argument 1 Firstly, smartphones are useful learning tools. Students can look up word meanings, read e-books, and join online quizzes. As a result, learning becomes more interesting.

Argument 2 Secondly, smartphones help students stay safe. Parents can contact their children in an emergency. Therefore, a phone gives peace of mind.

Reiteration In conclusion, smartphones can support learning and safety. Thus, schools should allow them with proper rules.
